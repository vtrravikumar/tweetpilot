# Vichar Branding

## Core identity

**Vichar — By VTRRK**

Vichar is derived from the Sanskrit **विचार (Vicāra)**.

**Meaning:** thought, reflection, consideration, deliberation, idea.

The product idea is **from thought to expression**: Vichar helps a user turn an idea into concise expression while keeping the user in control.

## Sanskrit brand signature

**विचारय। आकारय। स्वकीयं कुरु।**

**Think it. Shape it. Make it yours.**

- **विचारय। (Vicāraya.)** — Think it / contemplate it.
- **आकारय। (Ākāraya.)** — Shape it / give it form.
- **स्वकीयं कुरु। (Svakīyaṃ kuru.)** — Make it your own.

## Usage guidance

Use the Sanskrit signature and the meaning of **विचार (Vicāra)** in suitable Vichar brand surfaces, especially:

- Vichar website and landing page
- About/brand explanation
- Launch and product storytelling
- Future marketing material
- Suitable extension onboarding or about surfaces

Keep the core English identity consistent:

**Vichar — By VTRRK**  
**From thought to expression.**

The Sanskrit should reinforce the identity, not replace the clear English product description.

## Brand principle

Vichar is not about replacing the user's voice with AI. It is about helping a thought become an expression — with the user reviewing, shaping and owning the final words.
